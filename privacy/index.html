Nell Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Nell is made by Louis-Philippe Cote. In short: the app has no account, no server and no ads, and it does not collect any data about you. Your logs and your nail photos stay on your phone.

What the app uses, on your phone only

  • Camera. To take your daily nail photo. To take it by itself, the app looks for your hand in the camera's picture with Apple's on-device hand detection. That picture is never saved or sent anywhere: only the photo you choose to keep is saved, on your phone.
  • Photos. Only if you pick a photo instead of taking one. iOS shows its own picker and gives the app just the photo you pick, not your library.
  • Notifications. A daily reminder to take your nail photo, at the time you choose. It is scheduled on your phone.
  • Widgets, Control Center, Shortcuts and Siri. To log a bite or an urge from outside the app. They write to the same storage on your phone as the app.

What the app stores

Your bites, urges, their reasons and notes, your daily nail photos (and the original of a photo you crop), Nell's progress and your settings. They are stored on your phone, in the app's own storage shared with its widgets. Like any app's data, they are part of your phone's backups (iCloud or computer) if you turned those on.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.
  • No data, photos included, sent to the developer.

Your choices

You can turn the camera or notifications off in iOS Settings at any time. You can delete a photo in the app, or erase everything with Settings > Start over. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Children

Nell does not collect any personal information, from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and its date changed.
